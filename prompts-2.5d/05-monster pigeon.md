# 05 — Monster — Pigeon

Monstro leve do beco. Cola o bloco abaixo no gerador, sem imagem de entrada. O nome e o beco ficam neste arquivo. No prompt entra só o bicho.

O ciclo que vale é o voo. Anexa o still aprovado e cola o bloco. Sem still, não gera. A sheet de chão, só com cabeça e pé, não leu no tamanho do jogo. A plaquinha de papelão nas costas também não entra: o voo ficou sem ela.

## Still

```text
Solid flat uniform chromakey green screen background (#00FF00) filling the entire image edge to edge. Every background pixel is the same flat unlit green. No gradient, no blur, no bokeh, no vignette, no street, no room, no dark backdrop. One single view, not a turnaround, not two panels.

One stylized 3D game character floating in empty space. Full body, hand-painted soft shading, light on the character only from the upper left, chunky silhouette readable at small size. Small neon magenta and cyan marks on the body only. Neutral standing pose, facing three-quarter toward the camera, feet visible and fully inside the frame, centered, with padding around the silhouette. Absolutely no floor, no ground plane, no display base, no pedestal, no podium, no rubble pile, no contact shadow, no text. The character is: a plump street pigeon with a puffed chest, messy gray feathers, an iridescent neck, stubby legs, and an annoyed look, compact and wide
```

Recusa se o fundo for escuro, borrado ou um beco, se ele estiver em cima de uma base, ou se o pé estiver cortado. Ciano e magenta no corpo são figurino. Verde no corpo vaza no corte.

## Voo

Anexa o still aprovado. O corpo fica na célula. O jogo é que anda. Seis células iguais, numa fileira só. O corpo sobe e desce muito, com as asas abertas. Um balanço curto é sheet falha.

```text
Solid flat uniform chromakey green screen background (#00FF00) filling every cell edge to edge. Flat unlit green only. Not white, not black, not dark, not transparent, not a copy of the reference backdrop. No street, no blur, no base, no pedestal, no floor, no contact shadow. No text, no captions, no pose numbers, no words. Spritesheet of the reference character only. Keep the same design, colors, proportions, and painted style. Exactly six equal square cells in one single horizontal row. Not two rows. Not a pose chart. One complete cycle, no extra cells. Full body in every cell, wings fully inside the cell. The chest faces the same three-quarter angle in every cell, matching the reference. The camera does not turn. Do not rotate the body into a side profile. The whole body rises and falls between cells. Wings stay open. Neighboring cells show a different height, obvious when the character is small. Crop and upper-left light on the character identical across frames. Seamless loop, no travel across the cell. The motion is: an in-place hover flight with big readable changes. The chest keeps the same three-quarter angle in every pose. Do not turn the body to side profile. The whole body rises and falls. Pose 1: body low. Pose 2: body lifted high. Pose 3: body still high, starting to descend. Pose 4: body low again. Pose 5: body lifted high. Pose 6: body descends back to the low pose, matching pose 1. The body travels a long way up and down. A slight bob is not enough. The body stays centered in the cell, no sideways drift
```

Recusa se as seis células forem o mesmo still, se ele virar de perfil, se só a cabeça ou o pé mexer, se o corpo mal subir, ou se ele andar para o lado dentro da célula.
