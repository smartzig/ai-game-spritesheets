# 04 — Walk video, west only — Pinduca

The walk prompt is the one that produced the final in-game art. The idle prompt uses that same plate, so a standing pose can be extracted without touching the loop.

## Walk (copy & paste)

```text
Full-body character asset for video game 2D sprite sheet extraction. The anthropomorphic cat character walks in place facing completely sideways to the left in a strict side profile view (perfil lateral), looking directly to the left instead of toward the camera. Frame one is already this left profile. Do not show a T-pose. Do not turn from the front. Ela caminha com passos pequenos, braços abaixados ao lado do corpo com balanço extremamente sutil e suave, quase imóveis, sem movimentos exagerados. Floating in mid-air void with absolutely no floor, no ground plane, no floor surface. Solid flat uniform chromakey green screen background (#00FF00) filling the entire frame. Completely flat unlit environment, zero ground shadows, zero floor contact shadows, zero ambient occlusion. Perfectly centered with generous padding around ears, tail, and paws. Seamless looping in-place walk cycle.
```

## Idle (copy & paste, same plate)

Full-body character asset for video game 2D sprite sheet extraction. Frame one is already this left profile. Do not show a T-pose. Pés plantados, sem passo. Braços abaixados ao lado do corpo, quase imóveis, só uma respiração mínima. Floating in mid-air void with absolutely no floor, no ground plane, no floor surface. Solid flat uniform chromakey green screen background (#00FF00) filling the entire frame.
